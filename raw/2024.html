President

I am a person living with severe hemophilia B. Over 30 years, I have served the Bleeding Disorder Community in various positions. I serve as the Treasurer of the local chapter for two years in the early 1990’s and then as President for two years. I was elected as a National Hemophilia Foundation (NHF) Regional Director to the NHF Board of Directors and served as VP of Chapter Affairs for two years. I then serve as President of NHF Board of Directors for two years. In the early 2000’s I serve on the interim Board of Directors for NHF after the previous board voted to dissolve itself. I was elected to the NHF Board of Directors and served two terms as Vice Chairperson and then served three terms as Chairperson of the NHF Board of Directors. During this 30-year period I have also served on numerous committees and currently serve on two working groups for the National Bleeding Disorders Foundation (NBDF formerly NHF). The skills I bring to the Lone Star Bleeding Disorders Foundation (LSBDF) are an understanding of leadership and service to the community. I currently serve as the 2nd Vice President on the LSBDF Board of Directors; I am seeking your support to be the next President of the LSBDF.
Raymond Stanhope

I am bilingual and support the work of the foundation with the Spanish speaking community and I believe that the foundation should continue its outreach to the unengaged communities within the foundations' service area. I love meeting new people and sharing information about new therapies and the resources of the foundation with them. I am seeking your support in being the next 2nd Vice President on the LSBDF Board of Directors.
